# Glass and chrome

A single mark, letter or plain form in a material that does something
optical: clear or frosted glass, mirror chrome, holographic foil, liquid
pearl, or glass lit from inside. It usually sits on a dark or deep-coloured
tile. It suits AI tools, wallets, photo and wallpaper apps, and any app
about clarity, focus or light, and it is strongest when the material is the
idea. For a physical product, use Hardware or Soft 3D object. If nothing
shows through the form, mirrors in it or glows inside it, the icon is a
System glyph.

## Anchors

- Base keeps all its colour inside one tilted slab of blue glass on white, ultramarine to icy cyan.
- Vegas Craps makes the tile a translucent red die, so the far pips show through as ghosts.
- Widgetik overlaps frosted amber, magenta and teal panels on slate and lets the colours mix where they cross.
- Glow Space puts a light inside a glass hexagon and lets the walls catch it.
- Retro (Photos with Friends) draws a split letter in black mirror chrome with nothing but its reflections.
- Sugar presses a smile into a die-cut holographic-foil speech bubble, rainbow facets breaking across it.
- Partiful makes its P from liquid pearl on near-black aubergine, with sheen and film grain but no hard highlight.
- Habit Tracker Ripples braids a frosted ribbon into a ring that is white at the top and takes the violet tile's colour lower down. This is the Liquid Glass end of the style.

## Rendering standard

Pick one material per set and put it in place of `[MATERIAL]`. Coloured
glass is "thick clear [colour] glass, colour inside the body, deepest near
the light and palest where light leaves it". Frosted glass is "frosted
milk-white glass, nearly opaque at the top and tinted by the tile toward
the bottom, blurring what lies behind it". Chrome is "mirror chrome
reflecting one white softbox band and a dark horizon, reflections
hard-edged along the curvature". Foil is "high-gloss holographic foil in
broad spectral facets that break at creases". Pearl is "satin liquid pearl
with a pale cyan-to-lilac sheen". Inner light is "glass lit from inside, a
white core fading through [colour] to a saturated rim".

> Rendering: one form in [MATERIAL], physically based and studio clean, at
> the craft level of the best iOS 26 icons. The material draws the form by
> what passes through it, reflects in it or glows inside it, never by
> outlines or painted shading. Real thickness, shown by a few degrees of
> tilt or a visible side, with a thin bright refracting rim on every edge.
> One soft key light from the upper left, one specular highlight on the
> upper-left edge. Flawless surfaces, no scratches, bubbles, droplets, dust
> or sparkles. The form sits frontal and centred on the tile with no cast
> shadow, floor or reflection beneath it. The tile is [TILE], flat or a
> two-stop vertical gradient lighter at the top, with fine film grain and
> nothing else. One hue family, every colour as hex. No text unless named.

## Composition

The form covers 55 to 80 percent of the tile's width, usually 65 to 75.
Partiful covers 54, Base 64, Retro 69, Glow Space 74, Widgetik 77.

Either the form sits centred and frontal on the tile, or the tile is the
glass and the material runs edge to edge, as in Vegas Craps and Pool. Never
half-fill.

Use one form, or up to three pieces of one material, like Widgetik's
panels. Use two materials only when the pairing is the idea, like Obsidian
Web Clipper's chrome clip through a frosted pocket.

The silhouette is plain and heavy, with rounded corners. Strokes are at
least 7 percent of the tile's width, because thin glass has nothing to
refract with. Detail lives inside the material, in what bends, reflects or
glows, never on the outline. Show the thickness. Base tilts 3 degrees to
bare its lower and left edges.

Build in one overlap where something shows through, like Financial's
smoked F over a card stripe or Mapkin's frosted bubble blurring the map
behind it.

The background only sets the colour. It is flat, or two stops lighter at
the top: Ripples #5a76ff to #1b00be, Glow Space #262626 to #131313. Put
glowing or coloured glass on near-black, frosted white on a saturated
mid-tone, and chrome or deep-coloured glass on white. Corner's
photographed sky is the one exception, because chrome needs something to
reflect.

## Colour

Use one hue family in three to five values, plus the tile's neutral and at
most one accent the idea needs. Glass runs the full value range of its hue.
Base goes #19289c, #0f20e7, #008aff, #68d1ff, #bef2fe from thick to thin,
and Glow Space puts #1045a3, #0099ec and #64e9eb on #1f1e1b. Chrome has no
colour of its own. Retro is #23221f, #858480 and #c0bfba. Spectral colour
belongs to foil, on a neutral ground like Sugar's #dcdce6. Frosted glass
inverts all this. The tile carries the hue, Radiance's #10a0ec, and the
glass is white tinted by it, #cfebfe and #a9cbf5. A brand colour goes
inside the glass as its deepest tone, or into the tile under frosted glass.
It never goes onto chrome.

## Never

- An outline around the form. Edges are a bright rim or a reflection, never ink.
- Clear glass with nothing in it or behind it. It renders as a grey smear.
- Chrome drawn as a smooth grey gradient. That is plastic. Chrome needs hard-edged dark and light reflections.
- A second highlight, star glints, lens flares, sparkles or bokeh.
- Rainbow fringes and chromatic aberration on plain glass. The generator adds them unasked.
- Scratches, bubbles, droplets or dust. Cassette's scratched glass is how this style looked in 2012.
- A floor, a horizon, or a shadow or reflection under the form.
- A room, a city or a tiny world in the reflection or inside the glass.
- The stock 3D look: toy plastic, default HDRI lighting, one bevel on every edge.
- Text nobody asked for. For a wordmark, spell the word out letter by letter.

## Example prompt

    App icon for Stillpoint, a Mac focus timer that blurs everything except
    the task in hand.

    Subject: one thick glass slab with softly rounded corners, about 68% of
    the tile's width, tilted 4 degrees clockwise so its left and bottom
    edges show. A perfectly clear round lens, a third of the slab's width,
    is set into its upper right. Behind the slab, on the tile, sits one
    amber disc (#ffb03a), slightly larger than the lens. Through the frosted
    glass it is a soft glowing smear, and through the lens it is sharp and
    bright. Nothing else.

    Rendering: one form in frosted milk-white glass, nearly opaque at the
    top and tinted by the tile toward the bottom, blurring what lies behind
    it, physically based and studio clean, at the craft level of the best
    iOS 26 icons. The material draws the form by what passes through it,
    reflects in it or glows inside it, never by outlines or painted
    shading. Real thickness, shown by a few degrees of tilt or a visible
    side, with a thin bright refracting rim on every edge. One soft key
    light from the upper left, one specular highlight on the upper-left
    edge. Flawless surfaces, no scratches, bubbles, droplets, dust or
    sparkles. The form sits frontal and centred on the tile with no cast
    shadow, floor or reflection beneath it. The tile is deep indigo,
    #2b2f8c at the top to #15123f at the bottom, with fine film grain and
    nothing else. Indigo plus the one amber accent, every colour as hex.
    No text.

    Transparent background: a real alpha channel outside the icon tile, no
    backdrop, no checkerboard, no cast shadow. Output a PNG with transparency.
