# Scene and collage

The icon is a picture, not a symbol: a place with depth, a printed plate, a few stickers, or a paper thing seen close. It suits apps about a time, a place or a held artefact, such as sleep, reading, travel, sport, games, photos, receipts and parcels. When the app is a tool, choose an object or a mark instead. Pick one family per set and name it in every prompt. An illustrated scene suits states and places. A vintage print suits collections, nature and anything with a past. A sticker or photo collage suits apps built on the user's own pictures and music. A paper object suits apps that replace a receipt, box, page or card, and the tile becomes that thing.

## Anchors

- BetterSleep puts a moon two thirds of the width behind a tilted hill, with one dark sprout on the crest.
- ShutEye is one blue hue, an off-centre crescent and a single glowing dune ridge.
- Lamplight lights a brass lamp from its own bulb against a dim, desaturated bookcase.
- EPL Live sits the ball on the touchline under a night sky that darkens toward the horizon.
- Art of Fauna crops a hand-coloured engraving of a duck to its head and keeps the hatching.
- Package Tracker is the face of a kraft box with one tape strip and one label.
- Spenzy pins a crumpled receipt to cork, with one red pen circle as the only colour.
- Airbuds slaps three stickers on a white case lid, two of them cut by the edge.
- Okuri is one white page with one red kanji, peeling from a corner.

## Rendering standard

For an illustrated scene:

> Rendering: a finished editorial illustration with real depth, painted like a picture-book jacket. Three planes only: ground running off the bottom and sides, one focal subject on or rising behind it, a plain field of sky or wall above. Flat shapes with soft painted gradients, no outlines. One light source inside the picture, such as a moon, a lamp or a sun hidden by the subject, gives the subject a soft halo; everything else steps down into shade. Shadows are a darker, cooler step of the local colour, never grey. Fine monochrome grain, strongest on lit surfaces and ground, almost none in dark sky. A low horizon, curved or tilted a few degrees, with the subject overlapping it. Full bleed to every edge, no border, frame, vignette or glass rim. One hue family from dark to light; the subject is the brightest value or the single accent, the background darker and greyer.

For the other families, keep the shadow, grain, bleed and colour sentences and replace the rest with one of these. Vintage print: "a hand-coloured copperplate engraving from a natural history plate, hatching left visible, thin watercolour washes, cream rag paper", or "a three-colour risograph, grainy flat ink, slight misregistration". Collage: "real photographs cut out as stickers with white die-cut borders of one width, laid on one surface, a tight soft shadow at every overlap, one shared film grain". Paper object: "the tile is the face of the artefact, straight on in soft overhead light, real paper with tooth and creases, printed in one ink".

## Composition

A scene fills the tile, a paper object is the tile, and collage pieces sit on one surface that fills it.

- One focal subject at 35 to 70 percent of the width. BetterSleep's moon is 66, EPL Live's ball 65, ShutEye's crescent 35.
- Calm scenes set the subject off-centre: ShutEye's moon upper right, Art of Fauna's eye near the right third. Sport and game scenes centre it.
- The horizon sits 55 to 78 percent down and never runs level across the middle. BetterSleep's hill climbs from 77 to 65 percent, ShutEye's ridge is an S, My Little Universe bends it into a planet.
- The subject overlaps the horizon so the planes stack: moon behind hill, ball on the line, lamp on the shelf in front of the books. Nothing floats.
- Crop past the edge. Art of Fauna cuts the duck at two edges and Conduct THIS! runs the train off the right and bottom. A whole scene with margin round it is a postcard.
- Detail lives on the subject and the lit ground. The sky stays a plain gradient, and stars stay pinpoints and few: about thirty in BetterSleep, eight in ShutEye.
- Collage holds three or four pieces: one dominant at 50 to 80 percent of the width, the rest cut by the edge, tilts under 15 degrees. Deco and Lagguy reach six or eight only because collage is their product.
- A paper object fills the tile or is cropped by it, with three elements at most. Package Tracker's tape strip is 17 percent wide and its label a quarter. Incidental text becomes grey bars and a barcode. Only words that are the idea get spelled, like Okuri's kanji.

## Colour

Three to five colours, one hue family from dark to light plus at most one accent. The largest field carries the hue and the subject takes the extreme value or the accent. ShutEye is blues from #001848 to #0090d8 with a #ffffff moon. Package Tracker is one kraft hue in four values, #fde3c8 down to #b4612a. Spenzy is paper #e6e5e0, cork #9d8567, slate #373b45 and one red, #e53432. Art of Fauna is cream #f8dfc5 under earth inks #303925 and #af6d39. A brand colour takes the largest field or the accent, not both. Collage photos keep their own colour, and one sticker carries the brand.

## Never

- The generic sunset: half-sunk sun, level horizon, orange-to-purple sky, silhouetted palms.
- A landscape with no focal subject, or two subjects of equal weight.
- Hills behind hills behind hills. Three planes is the limit.
- Sparkles, confetti or bokeh across the sky. They are what cheapen ScreenSense and Soundmap.
- The scene shrunk into a framed window or badge with margin inside the tile.
- A floating subject. Everything stands, lies or hangs on something.
- Faceless vector people, isometric offices, outlines on every shape.
- HDR, lens flare, god rays, studio product lighting.
- Text nobody asked for: signage, captions, fake logos, gibberish receipts. Spell every word or ask for grey bars.
- A transparency checkerboard inside the picture, as in Deco. The generator bakes it in.
- Fake age on a print: coffee stains, burnt edges, a sepia wash.

## Example prompt

    Tidewell, tide times for sea swimmers. One orange float on flat dawn
    water, cut in half by the waterline, because the waterline is what the app
    watches.

    Rendering: a finished editorial illustration with real depth, painted like
    a picture-book jacket. Three planes only: ground running off the bottom
    and sides, one focal subject on or rising behind it, a plain field of sky
    or wall above. Flat shapes with soft painted gradients, no outlines. One
    light source inside the picture, such as a moon, a lamp or a sun hidden by
    the subject, gives the subject a soft halo; everything else steps down
    into shade. Shadows are a darker, cooler step of the local colour, never
    grey. Fine monochrome grain, strongest on lit surfaces and ground, almost
    none in dark sky. A low horizon, curved or tilted a few degrees, with the
    subject overlapping it. Full bleed to every edge, no border, frame,
    vignette or glass rim. One hue family from dark to light; the subject is
    the brightest value or the single accent, the background darker and
    greyer.

    Subject: a split view at water level. The sea surface crosses 58 percent
    of the way down, tilted three degrees up to the right. One bright orange
    #ff6a2b swim float sits right of centre, 45 percent of the width, half
    above the water and half below. Above, an empty dawn sky from #f2d6c2 at
    the waterline to #9fb4c6 at the top, the low sun hidden behind the float
    so its rim glows. Below, clear water from #3f8a86 to #123b42, the float's
    lower half slightly refracted. Two thin ripple rings on the surface. No
    birds, boats, coastline or text.

    Transparent background: a real alpha channel outside the icon tile, no
    backdrop, no checkerboard, no cast shadow. Output a PNG with transparency.
