# Mono mark

One symbol drawn as a logo on a quiet ground: black on white, white on
near-black, or one saturated colour on white. Depth, if any, stops at a lit
rim inside the mark's edge, a fine grain and one soft shadow. Twelve of its
28 corpus icons are Productivity apps, mostly dictation, AI, timer and to-do
tools; finance, news and health make up most of the rest. Choose it when the
app does one thing that can be drawn as a sign. Choose Lettermark when the
name is the asset and its letter carries the idea (Savant, Notedrafts). A
mark that grows from a letter but reads as a shape first stays here (Fuse's
metaball r, Matter's node-graph M). If it still means something to someone
who can't read the name, it's a Mono mark. A tactile app wants Soft 3D
object or Hardware, and one that needs a face with feelings wants Mascot.

## Anchors

- Things take time draws crossed t's as one calligraphic tally in pure
  black on pure white, with no shading at all.
- Chef crops a black rat in a chef's hat at the bottom edge and cuts every
  detail in white.
- DICE gives a square legs, eyes and a paper-cut edge, a character drawn as
  a logo.
- Thoughts punches quote marks through a grained charcoal bubble and adds
  one soft shadow, the most depth the style takes.
- Emphasis runs a lit rim just inside the edge of three black leaves, the
  inner bevel at its cleanest.
- Neuecast shrinks a white bar chart to a fifth of the width and parks it
  low right on near-black, so the emptiness is the composition.
- Offsuit makes twelve white-to-lavender spokes on flat #242329 read as one
  silhouette.
- Manus AI draws the act, a black hand mid-snap with three motion ticks,
  where the category would reach for a sparkle.
- Caltrak sets off-white petals on a green-black that falls to black, the
  tinted dark ground.

## Rendering standard

Pick one finish per set. Soft bevel, below, is the default. For flat ink
(Chef, DICE, Offsuit), replace the bevel and shadow sentences with
"perfectly flat ink, no shading, as if screen-printed". For a dark ground
(Caltrak, Neuecast), the ground becomes "near-black, #2a2a2a at the top left
falling to #121212 at the lower right" and the ink off-white #f2f2f2. A
saturated ink adds "a two-stop vertical gradient within its hue, the top
15% lighter".

> Rendering: one symbol drawn as a logo by an identity designer, with the
> restraint of a Paul Rand or Pentagram mark, not an illustration. A single
> solid silhouette in [ink], bold, frontal and flat, strokes at least a
> twentieth of the width with round ends, detail only as negative space cut
> out of it. Matte ink with a soft inner bevel, a narrow lighter rim just
> inside the edge, brightest along the top, and a flat interior. Light from
> directly above. One soft, short, faint shadow straight below, as if the
> mark were an enamel inlay barely proud of the surface. The ground is the
> whole face, a smooth vertical gradient from pure white at the top to
> #ebebeb at the bottom with a barely visible fine grain. Nothing else: no
> other colour, no frame, no inner panel, no text.

## Composition

- A centred mark spans 56 to 72% of the width (Manus 56, Thoughts 58,
  Emphasis 61, Copilot 64, Typeless 67, Offsuit 70, Utter 72). Aim for 60
  to 68. A tall mark may take 80% of the height (DICE). Centre the bounding
  box.
- The quiet variant sets a mark 15 to 25% wide on a third and leaves the
  rest empty. Neuecast's centre sits 64% across and 64% down; Typo's
  asterisk sits top right. It needs flat ink on a flat ground. A small mark
  dead centre looks unfinished.
- Bleed only with flat ink, when the crop says "poster" (Chef).
- The mark sits on the face. The ground is paper, never an object.
- One silhouette, strokes 5 to 7% of the width. Repeated parts count as one
  when they read as one figure (Offsuit's spokes, Utter's bars). A second
  element only if much smaller, like Countly's arrow badge.
- Detail lives in counters and cuts, never drawn on top. Motion ticks only
  when the gesture is the idea, like Manus's snapping fingers.
- Name the mark's shapes in the prompt (a crescent, a forked tail) so the
  generator draws a silhouette, not clip art.
- The ground does one thing: flat, or one gentle gradient (white to #ebebeb
  downward, #2a2a2a to #121212 across, or Mist's faint lift at the foot).
  Texture only if it means something and sits close to the ground's value.
  Placify's map streets are 6% off it, Copilot's hatch 4%.

## Colour

Two colours, ground and ink. A third only as one accent, on the whole mark
(Copilot #227bf8) or on one small element (Countly's #32af81 arrow, Focus
Timer's #fe4a69 strip). Inks run from true black to warm near-black (Fuse
#030202, Thoughts #2f2e2b, Utter #3d3c39). Light grounds are white to
#ebebeb, or a warm off-white for a paper brand (Placify #fef6ee). Dark
grounds keep a hint of hue: Offsuit #242329 violet, Mist #0c1018 navy,
Caltrak #25362e green. Accents are fully saturated and their gradient holds
the hue (Copilot #408cfc to #0161f3). A black mark may ramp toward grey down
its height, never past #6e6e6e (Bevel). The brand colour goes on the mark
over a neutral ground, or tints a dark ground under a white mark (Caltrak).

From the index: Manus AI #ebebeb, #292929, #858480. Offsuit #222222,
#dedeee, #9b9aa4. Copilot #227bf8, #eaedf2, #a5c0e2, #60bdff. Countly
#e0f8eb, #1f1e1b, #32af81.

## Never

- The category's stock symbol, like a microphone for dictation or a sparkle
  for AI. Draw the act, as Manus draws a snap of the fingers.
- A line icon of thin, even outlines. That is a system glyph.
- A picture: a scene, a horizon, an object in perspective.
- Chrome, glass, metal or reflections.
- A long shadow, a hard offset shadow, or a glow on a light ground.
- Extra parts: sparkles, dots, orbit rings, speed lines.
- A frame or inner panel around the mark.
- A mockup: a business card, embossed paper, a wall sign.
- Text: the app's name, a tagline, "LOGO".
- Muddy greys: black drifting to mid-grey, white going beige, grain that
  reads as dirt.
- A second accent, or a gradient across hues.

## Example prompt

    Tern, an app that alerts you when your flight's gate or time changes. A
    tern seen from below in a steep banking turn, one solid silhouette of
    swept wings and a deep forked tail, angled down to the right like an
    arrival arrow. The only detail is the white gap between the tail's
    streamers. The bird spans about 64% of the icon's width, its bounding
    box centred.

    Rendering: one symbol drawn as a logo by an identity designer, with the
    restraint of a Paul Rand or Pentagram mark, not an illustration. A
    single solid silhouette in warm near-black #1c1b19, bold, frontal and
    flat, strokes at least a twentieth of the width with round ends, detail
    only as negative space cut out of it. Matte ink with a soft inner bevel,
    a narrow lighter rim just inside the edge, brightest along the top, and
    a flat interior. Light from directly above. One soft, short, faint
    shadow straight below, as if the mark were an enamel inlay barely proud
    of the surface. The ground is the whole face, a smooth vertical gradient
    from pure white at the top to #ebebeb at the bottom with a barely
    visible fine grain. Nothing else: no other colour, no frame, no inner
    panel, no text.

    Transparent background: a real alpha channel outside the icon tile, no
    backdrop, no checkerboard, no cast shadow. Output a PNG with transparency.
